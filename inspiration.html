NovaPay · Feature inspiration

Not sure what to build? Start here.

Every real bank feature starts with one question: who is this for, and what is hard for them right now? Pick a card, change it, make it yours. There are no wrong answers.

How to use these cards

  • Browse, or press Surprise me.
  • Pick an idea, then change one thing so it becomes yours (a different person, a twist, a new look).
  • Copy the starter prompt, then open the persona wizard to say who it is for.
  • Build in the blank NovaPay template. Not sure about the code? Use the starter template.

Remember: NovaPay is pretend. Use made-up names and numbers only. Never type real bank details, real names or real passwords into your page.

Idea cards

Make it yours: twist cards

Stuck on a card? Add one of these twists.

  • What if the person using it is 8 years old? Or 80?
  • What if they have very little time, or a very small screen?
  • What if they find numbers stressful?
  • What if they can’t see colours well, or use only a keyboard?
  • What would make it fun instead of boring?
  • What would make someone feel safe and in control of their money?
