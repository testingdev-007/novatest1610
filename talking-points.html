Good ground rules for everyone

  • There are no silly questions. “I don’t know, let’s find out” is a great answer.
  • Keep conversations in the room, in the open, with the group. No private chats.
  • Don’t share personal details such as home address, school gossip, passwords or social media accounts.
  • Listen as much as you talk. It’s fine to disagree politely.
  • Opinions about the work are fine. Opinions about people are not.
  • If anything makes you uncomfortable, tell your teacher or the lead for the day straight away.

People here speak for themselves, not for their employer. Nobody needs to share confidential work or customer information.
